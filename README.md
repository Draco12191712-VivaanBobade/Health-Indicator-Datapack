# Health Indicator Datapack
Health Indicator shows every player's health under their name tag and as hearts in the tab list, colour coded from green to red. Unlike other health indicators, it never gets stuck showing 0 for players at full health.

# _<span style="color: rgb(248, 202, 198);">Health</span> <span style="color: rgb(248, 202, 198);">Indicator Datapack!</span>_

    <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">This datapack lets you see anyone's <strong><span style="color: rgb(248, 202, 198);">health</span></strong> just by looking below their name! Unlike many other <span style="color: rgb(248, 202, 198);"><strong>health</strong></span> indicator datapacks, it doesnt get stuck at 0 hearts for people who are at full <span style="color: rgb(248, 202, 198);"><strong>health</strong></span>.</span>

## <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">Features</span>

*   <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="color: rgb(248, 202, 198);"><strong>Health</strong></span>: <strong><span style="color: rgb(248, 202, 198);">Health</span></strong> is displayed under the player's nametag. (e.g., 10 hearts above someone at full health.)</span>
*   <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">Hearts: You can also hold Tab to see everyone's <span style="color: rgb(248, 202, 198);"><strong>health</strong></span> at once! (You can turn this off if you don't want it.)</span>
*   <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">Colour coding: You can also check someone's <span style="color: rgb(248, 202, 198);"><strong>health</strong></span> without looking at the number.</span>
    *   <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="color: rgb(191, 237, 210);"><em>Green</em></span> means 7-10 hearts</span>
    *   <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="color: rgb(251, 238, 184);"><em>Yellow</em></span> means 3.5-6.5 hearts</span>
    *   <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="color: rgb(248, 202, 198);"><em>Red</em></span> means 0-3 hearts</span>
    *   <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="color: rgb(241, 196, 15);"><em>Gold</em></span> means people who have extra hearts (like a person after popping a totem or eating a Gapple)</span>

***

## <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">Supported Versions</span>

<span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">Works in <strong>Minecraft: Java Edition</strong>&nbsp;1.20.3 to 26.3! The same file works on them all.&nbsp;</span> 

<span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">Works in singleplayer, on vanilla servers, and on servers that support data packs (like <strong><span style="color: rgb(236, 202, 250);">Paper</span></strong> <em>and</em> <strong><span style="color: rgb(236, 202, 250);">Fabric</span></strong>).</span>

***

## ____Commands____

| <h2 style="padding-left: 80px;"><span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">Command</span></h2> |<h2 style="padding-left: 80px;"><span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">What it does</span></h2> |
| ------------------------------------- |------------------------------------------------- |
| <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><code>/function healthindicator:tablist_off</code></span> |<span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">Hides the hearts in the tab list</span> |
| <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><code>/function healthindicator:tablist_on</code></span> |<span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">Shows the hearts in the tab list again</span> |
| <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><code>/function healthindicator:uninstall</code></span> |<span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">Removes the health display completely (see below)</span> |

## <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">How to <strong><span style="color: rgb(224, 62, 45);">uninstall</span></strong></span>

1.  <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">Run <code>/function healthindicator:uninstall</code>.</span>
2.  <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">Delete <code>HealthIndicator.zip</code> from the <code>datapacks</code> folder.</span>
3.  <span style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">Run <code>/reload</code>.</span>
